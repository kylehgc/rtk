# Adopted contributors are credited in git, not on the landing page

This fork goes to unusual lengths to preserve authorship — cherry-pick over re-implementation, merge commits over squash, Amendments as separate commits — so the absence of a contributors list on the landing page looks like an oversight. It is deliberate.

The 17 people whose commits the fork carries wrote their PRs against **rtk-ai/rtk**. They have never heard of this fork and did not choose to be associated with it. Publishing their handles as the fork's contributors claims a participation none of them gave, and uses their names to make a fork look more credible than its own contributions warrant. Git authorship is the record, it is permanent, and it is what Apache 2.0's attribution requirement asks for; a roster on a marketing page is a separate claim, and nothing requires it.

So the Delta credits the **upstream PR** rather than the person. A reader who wants to know who wrote a fix clicks through and sees the author on their own PR, in the context where they chose to be public — and a PR number is stronger evidence than a name anyway, because it is checkable. Someone who opens a PR against the fork directly has opted in, and is named.
